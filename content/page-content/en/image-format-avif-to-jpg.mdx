AVIF is excellent for websites, but support outside the browser is still patchy: many photo editors, messaging apps, print services and upload forms reject it. Converting AVIF to JPG gives you a file that works everywhere.

## Why convert AVIF to JPG?

Save an AVIF from a website and you may find you cannot open or edit it. A JPG version fixes that. The JPG will be larger than the AVIF, because JPG compression is less efficient, and any transparent areas are filled with white.

## Frequently asked questions

<FAQ question="Why can I not open my AVIF file?">
  AVIF is a recent format. Browsers display it, but many desktop programs, older
  phones and online services do not support it yet.
</FAQ>

<FAQ question="Why is the JPG bigger than the AVIF?">
  AVIF compresses much more efficiently than JPG. The JPG needs more bytes to
  keep a similar quality.
</FAQ>

<FAQ question="Are my images uploaded?">
  No. The conversion runs entirely in your browser, on your device. Your images
  are never uploaded to a server, so this works for private and confidential
  files too.
</FAQ>
