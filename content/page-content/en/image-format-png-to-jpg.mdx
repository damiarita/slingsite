PNG is lossless, which makes photos and screenshots saved as PNG very large. Converting them to JPG can shrink them several times over, and JPG is accepted by every app, form and website. Drop your PNG files and get JPG versions in seconds.

## Why convert PNG to JPG?

Many upload forms limit file size or only accept JPG. Photos saved as PNG waste space for no visible benefit. Keep in mind that JPG has no transparency: transparent areas are filled with white, so for logos and icons with a transparent background WebP is a better target.

## Frequently asked questions

<FAQ question="What happens to the transparent background of my PNG?">
  JPG cannot store transparency, so transparent pixels are filled with white. To
  keep the transparency, convert the PNG to WebP or AVIF instead.
</FAQ>

<FAQ question="Why is the JPG so much smaller than the PNG?">
  PNG stores every pixel exactly, while JPG uses lossy compression tuned for
  photos. For photographic content the difference is barely visible but the file
  is much lighter.
</FAQ>

<FAQ question="Are my images uploaded?">
  No. The conversion runs entirely in your browser, on your device. Your images
  are never uploaded to a server, so this works for private and confidential
  files too.
</FAQ>
