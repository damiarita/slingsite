1. Optional: open Advanced Settings to resize by percentage, width or height, and add more sizes.
1. Click Start Conversion, then download each file or all of them at once. Nothing is uploaded: the conversion runs in your browser.

## Why convert WebP to JPG?

JPG is the most compatible image format in existence. Converting your WebP files makes them easy to edit, print, attach to emails or upload anywhere. If the WebP has transparent areas, they are filled with white, because JPG does not support transparency.
