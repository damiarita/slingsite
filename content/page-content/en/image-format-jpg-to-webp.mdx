## Why convert JPG to WebP?

Google recommends serving next-gen formats, and WebP is the most widely supported one. Smaller photos load faster on mobile, improve your Largest Contentful Paint and reduce hosting bandwidth. Keep the original JPGs as a fallback if you support very old browsers.
