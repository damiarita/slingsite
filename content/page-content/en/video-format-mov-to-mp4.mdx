MOV is Apple QuickTime format, used by iPhones and Macs. Many Windows apps, Android phones, websites and editors handle MP4 more reliably. This converter re-encodes your MOV files to H.264 MP4 directly in your browser.

## How to convert MOV to MP4 (H.264)

1. Drop your MOV files in the box above, or click to pick them. You can add as many as you like.
1. MP4 (H.264) is already selected as the output format. Switch on other formats if you want them too.
1. Optional: open Advanced Settings to resize by percentage, width or height, and add more sizes.
1. Click Start Conversion, then download each file or all of them at once. Nothing is uploaded: the conversion runs in your browser.

## Why convert MOV to MP4 (H.264)?

MP4 is the safest format to share or publish a video. iPhone MOV files often use HEVC, which some devices cannot play; converting to H.264 fixes that.
