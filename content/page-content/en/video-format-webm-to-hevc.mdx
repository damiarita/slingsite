## Why convert WebM (VP9) to HEVC (H.265)?

Use it to move WebM clips into the Apple ecosystem without bloating them. HEVC encoding requires support in your browser and hardware, available in Safari and in Chrome or Edge on many recent devices; otherwise pick H.264 MP4.

## Frequently asked questions

<FAQ question="Why choose HEVC instead of H.264?">
  HEVC files are typically much smaller than H.264 at similar quality. H.264
  plays on more devices, so choose it when maximum compatibility matters.
</FAQ>

<FAQ question="Why does the conversion fail?">
  HEVC encoding is only available when your device provides an encoder. Safari
  on a Mac, or Chrome and Edge on recent hardware, usually work.
</FAQ>

<FAQ question="Are my videos uploaded?">
  No. The video is decoded and encoded in your browser, on your device. Nothing
  is sent to a server, which also means there is no file size limit other than
  your device memory.
</FAQ>
