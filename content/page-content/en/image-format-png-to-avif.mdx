AVIF can be many times smaller than a PNG of the same image, and it keeps transparency. That makes it a great choice for product photos on transparent backgrounds, illustrations and hero images. Convert your PNG files here with no software to install.

## Why convert PNG to AVIF?

PNG was designed for lossless storage, not for fast websites. Switching to AVIF gives you the best compression browsers support today while keeping the alpha channel. AVIF encoding is slower than PNG or WebP, so allow a little extra time for big images.

## Frequently asked questions

<FAQ question="Does AVIF support transparency?">
  Yes. AVIF has a full alpha channel, so transparent areas of your PNG stay
  transparent.
</FAQ>

<FAQ question="Should I choose AVIF or WebP for my PNGs?">
  AVIF usually gives smaller files; WebP encodes faster and is supported by
  slightly older browsers. You can switch on both formats and compare the
  results.
</FAQ>

<FAQ question="Are my images uploaded?">
  No. The conversion runs entirely in your browser, on your device. Your images
  are never uploaded to a server, so this works for private and confidential
  files too.
</FAQ>
