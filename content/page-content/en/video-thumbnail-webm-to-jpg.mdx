WebM is the open video format of the web, used by browser recordings, video sites and many web apps. JPG is the universal image format: it opens everywhere and keeps files small. This tool takes the first frame of each WebM video and saves it as a JPG, without uploading anything.

## How to extract a JPG thumbnail from WebM

1. Drop your WebM videos in the box above, or click to pick them. You can add as many as you like.
1. JPG is already selected as the output format. Switch on other formats if you want them too.
1. Optional: open Advanced Settings to resize the thumbnail by percentage, width or height, and add more sizes.
1. Click Extract Thumbnails, then download each image or all of them at once. Nothing is uploaded: everything runs in your browser.

## Why save WebM thumbnails as JPG?

If you publish WebM videos on your site, a poster image from the first frame makes the page look complete before the video loads, instead of showing an empty box. JPG is the safest choice for video thumbnails: video platforms, social networks, emails and content management systems all accept it, and the file stays light. Video frames have no transparency, so JPG loses nothing there.

## Frequently asked questions

<FAQ question="Which WebM videos are supported?">
  WebM files with VP8, VP9 or AV1 video, which modern browsers decode. Screen
  recordings made in the browser are usually VP8 or VP9.
</FAQ>

<FAQ question="Is a JPG thumbnail good enough?">
  Yes. The frame is saved with a high quality setting, so it looks like the
  video, at a fraction of the size of a PNG.
</FAQ>

<FAQ question="Are my videos uploaded?">
  No. The first frame is decoded and saved as an image in your browser, on your
  device. Your videos are never sent to a server, so this works for private
  files too.
</FAQ>
