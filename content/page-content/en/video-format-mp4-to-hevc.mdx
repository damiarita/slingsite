Most MP4 files use H.264. Re-encoding them with HEVC (H.265) keeps the MP4 container but uses a much more efficient codec, often producing files around half the size at similar quality. Handy for archiving and for sharing on Apple devices.

1. Click Start Conversion, then download each file or all of them at once. Nothing is uploaded: the conversion runs in your browser.

## Why convert MP4 to HEVC (H.265)?

HEVC saves storage and bandwidth. Encoding needs HEVC support in your browser and hardware, available in Safari and in Chrome or Edge on many recent devices. Very low-bitrate MP4s may not shrink much, since there is little left to save.

## Frequently asked questions

<FAQ question="Will every device play the HEVC file?">
  Apple devices and most recent phones, TVs and computers play HEVC. Some older
  devices and Firefox do not, so keep the H.264 version if compatibility
  matters.
</FAQ>

<FAQ question="Why does the conversion fail?">
  Your browser could not find an HEVC encoder on this device. Try Safari, or
  Chrome or Edge on a more recent computer.
</FAQ>

<FAQ question="Are my videos uploaded?">
  No. The video is decoded and encoded in your browser, on your device. Nothing
  is sent to a server, which also means there is no file size limit other than
  your device memory.
</FAQ>
